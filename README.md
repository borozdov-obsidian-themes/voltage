# Borozdov Voltage

A theme from the Borozdov collection. Two faces — light **Sprig**, deep moss ink with lime
voltage on white paper, and dark **Thicket**, the same lime sparking on a near-black forest
floor. Forest-green ink, black-weight headlines, pills for everything you press and one
electric lime for what you act on.

![Borozdov Voltage in light mode](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/borozdov-obsidian-themes/voltage/main/screenshots/light.png)

![Borozdov Voltage in dark mode](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/borozdov-obsidian-themes/voltage/main/screenshots/dark.png)

## Principles

- **A loud, confident voice.** Voltage Sans Black for the title and the two largest
  headings, tightly tracked and stacked close; the platform's own sans for everything else.
- **Forest ink, never black.** Text, links and headings are a deep green-tinted ink; links
  keep an underline instead of a second colour.
- **One voltage.** Electric lime fills the main button, a checked task, a toggle and the
  open file; a pale linen mist carries tags and the plain note. By night the title itself
  sparks lime.
- **Pills and soft cards.** Every button and tag is a pill; callouts, code and tables are
  flat fog cards with 10px corners.

## Features

- Light and dark modes, following Settings → Appearance → Base color scheme
- Callouts as fog cards with the title in the type's colour; the plain note on a wash of
  green
- Tags as linen-mist pills that turn lime under the pointer
- A lime rule beside quotes and a lime highlighter
- Quiet editing: no focus ring around the note, its title or form fields while you type;
  property names read as labels, not boxed fields
- Text colours meet WCAG contrast on both faces
- The phone layout keeps the same colours and shapes
- No `!important`: every rule can be overridden with a CSS snippet

## Installation

**From the community directory:** Settings → Appearance → Themes → Manage, search for
**Borozdov Voltage**, then **Install and use**.

**By hand:** download `manifest.json` and `theme.css` from the [latest
release](https://github.com/borozdov-obsidian-themes/voltage/releases/latest) into
`<vault>/.obsidian/themes/Borozdov Voltage/`, then choose Borozdov Voltage under Settings →
Appearance → Themes.

## Font

Voltage Sans is embedded in `theme.css` as base64 WOFF2 under the SIL Open Font License
1.1 — see [`fonts/OFL.txt`](fonts/OFL.txt). It is a Latin and Cyrillic subset of Lato Black
(© 2011–2015 tyPoland Łukasz Dziedzic), renamed because a modified copy may not use the
original's Reserved Font Name. One weight, for the title and the two largest headings only.
